## Coverage gaps to carry forward

The suite is thirteen repeated, controlled task shapes, not a census of all But capabilities. Donated records broaden examples but lack final-state oracles and contain benchmark/development work. Before removing semantics in the following areas, either keep them available or design a small targeted evaluation: PR publication/authentication; worktree targeting; multiple contributors' changes; landed-branch dependency handling; alternate conflict modes; recovery from stale selectors after a partial chain; version mismatch; dirty state after a supplementary test; and final integration into an ordinary checkout.

No new experiment is running. These are proposed evidence gaps for the writing agent's eventual validation plan.
