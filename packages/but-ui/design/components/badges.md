# Badges

**Gray is the workhorse; colour is spent only when it means something.** Most
badges are `fillGray` or `lightGray`: counts and neutral labels that carry no
verdict. A coloured badge says what a thing is, not how much it matters (see
[Emphasis](../foundations/emphasis.md)), and pop is as rare on a badge as on a
button: at most one per surface. What each variant is for is on `Badge`.

**A badge is the soft weight of its colour.** The label in the colour over a
tint of it (see [Buttons](buttons.md)). A badge labels and a button acts, so a
pop badge is soft while the pop button is solid.
