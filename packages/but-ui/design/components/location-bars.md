# Location bars

The bar above a view's header: the window's controls, where the page sits,
then the state of what it shows.

**Back appears once the sidebar is folded away,** after the sidebar toggle;
while the sidebar shows, there's no Back. On a phone, with no sidebar to
toggle, Back leads alone.

**Its buttons match.** The sidebar toggle, Back and Refresh are ghost,
icon-only buttons of one size.

**Copies elsewhere are said in words.** When the branch or worktree is also on
other machines: "Also in 2 places", then "· 1 differs" in amber when a copy
differs, then the menu that opens them.
